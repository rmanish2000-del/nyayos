## 1. Executive Summary & Macro Pendency Landscape
The Indian justice system processes tens of millions of active disputes across a multi-tiered judicial and quasi-judicial framework. Total active pendency stands at approximately **58+ million cases**, heavily skewed towards subordinate courts.

## 2. Supreme Court of India
* **Role:** Apex constitutional court, court of record, and final court of appeal.

### Case Types
* **Special Leave Petitions (SLPs):** Under Article 136, challenging High Court orders (comprises ~70%+ of filings).
* **Writ Petitions:** Under Article 32 for direct enforcement of Fundamental Rights.
* **Appellate & Reference Cases:** Civil/Criminal statutory appeals and Presidential References.

### User Journey (SLP Lifecycle)
1. **Filing & Scrutiny:** E-filing portal submission, registry defect checks, and unique diary number generation.
2. **Listing & Oral Mentioning:** Mentioning before the Chief Justice's bench for urgent listing; routine listing via computer-generated roster.
3. **Admission Hearing:** Bench decides whether to issue notice or dismiss the SLP.
4. **Notice & Counter-Affidavit:** Service to respondents, written pleadings submission.
5. **Final Hearing & Judgment:** Comprehensive legal arguments leading to a reported judgment or dismissal.

* **Highest Volume Categories:** Criminal SLPs (bail/conviction challenges) and Service/Service-matter civil appeals.
* **Highest Pain Categories:** Interlocutory applications, admission-stage backlogs, and arbitrary admissions converting the Supreme Court into a routine court of appeal rather than a constitutional forum.

## 3. High Courts
* **Role:** Constitutional head of state judiciary, supervisory power over subordinate courts (Article 227), original and appellate civil/criminal jurisdiction.

### Case Types
* **Writ Petitions (Civil & Criminal):** Habeas corpus, mandamus, certiorari under Articles 226/227.
* **Appeals:** First and second appeals from District/Civil courts; criminal conviction appeals from Sessions Courts.
* **Company & Arbitration Petitions:** Commercial disputes and execution of awards.

### User Journey (Writ Petition Lifecycle)
1. **Drafting & E-Filing:** Petition uploaded with annexures, payment of court fees.
2. **Defect Removal & Listing:** Registry scrutiny; assigned to a single or division bench.
3. **Preliminary Hearing:** Motion hearing; interim stay granted or denied.
4. **Counter & Rejoinder:** State/private respondents file counter-affidavits.
5. **Final Hearing & Order/Judgment:** Dictation of judgment or detailed written order.

* **Highest Volume Categories:** Service matters (government employment disputes), land acquisition, and civil writ petitions.
* **Highest Pain Categories:** Second appeals from district courts, massive paper-book preparation requirements, and chronic judicial vacancies.

---

## 4. District & Subordinate Courts
* **Role:** Foundation of the trial system; direct interface for civil litigation and criminal prosecution.

### Case Types
* **Civil Suits:** Property partitions, money recovery, injunctions, specific performance, testamentary matters.
* **Criminal Trials:** Sessions trials (heinous crimes) and Magistrate trials (summary/warrant cases).
* **Execution Petitions:** Enforcing decrees and arbitral awards.

### User Journey (Civil Suit Lifecycle)
1. **Plaint Presentation & Admission:** Filing before the District Judge or Senior Civil Judge; summons issued.
2. **Service of Summons:** Process server delivery / registered post to the defendant.
3. **Written Statement & Framing of Issues:** Defendant files response; court frames legal/fact issues.
4. **Evidence & Cross-Examination:** Plaintiff and defendant witness testimony.
5. **Final Arguments & Judgment/Decree:** Final adjudication and decree drawing.

* **Highest Volume Categories:** Section 138 Negotiable Instruments Act (cheque bounce) cases, petty criminal offenses, and family/land property disputes.
* **Highest Pain Categories:** Witness non-attendance, repeated adjournments, physical file management, and long-pending execution proceedings.

## 5. Tribunals (NCLT, NGT, ITAT, CAT)
* **Role:** Specialized quasi-judicial bodies established to fast-track technical, corporate, tax, and administrative disputes.

### Case Types
* **Corporate & Insolvency (NCLT):** Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP), oppression and mismanagement.
* **Taxation (ITAT/CESTAT):** Income tax, customs, and excise duty disputes.
* **Administrative (CAT):** Central government employee service disputes.
* **Environment (NGT):** Environmental clearance challenges and pollution compensation.

### User Journey (NCLT Insolvency Application)
1. **Application Filing:** Creditor or corporate applicant e-files via MCA-21 portal / NCLT registry.
2. **Admission Stage:** Tribunal verifies default, checks documentation, and admits/rejects petition under IBC.
3. **Moratorium & IRP Appointment:** Public announcement, formation of Committee of Creditors (CoC).
4. **Resolution Plan Approval or Liquidation:** Commercial viability assessment and final order.

* **Highest Volume Categories:** Section 7 & Section 9 IBC applications (NCLT) and Income Tax appeals (ITAT).
* **Highest Pain Categories:** Frequent adjournment requests, quorum benches not sitting due to technical member vacancies, and prolonged corporate resolution timelines exceeding statutory limits.

## 6. Consumer Courts
* **Role:** Redressal of consumer grievances, unfair trade practices, and deficiency in services (District, State, and National Commissions).

### Case Types
* **Deficiency in Service:** Banking, insurance, medical negligence, real estate delays.
* **Product Liability:** Defective goods and hazardous manufacturing.

### User Journey (District Commission Complaint)
1. **Complaint Submission:** Physical or e-daakhil filing with requisite consumer welfare fee.
2. **Admissibility Review:** Commission checks jurisdiction and limitation period (2 years).
3. **Notice to Opposite Party (OP):** OP files written version within 30–45 days.
4. **Evidence via Affidavit:** Submission of written affidavits by both parties.
5. **Oral Argument & Order:** Summary trial closure and award of compensation.

* **Highest Volume Categories:** Real estate builder-buyer disputes, insurance claim rejections, and e-commerce/banking fraud complaints.
* **Highest Pain Categories:** Non-execution of consumer forum orders, vacant president/member posts in district commissions, and prolonged litigation turning summary trials into lengthy regular suits.

---

## 7. Revenue Courts
* **Role:** Adjudication of land records, mutations, agricultural land partitions, and revenue recovery (Tehsildar, Sub-Divisional Magistrate, Commissioner, Board of Revenue).

### Case Types
* **Mutation of Land:** Title updates following inheritance or sale.
* **Demarcation & Boundary Disputes:** Encroachments on agricultural or rural plots.
* **Tenancy & Land Ceiling:** Disputes under state-specific land reform acts.

### User Journey (Mutation/Partition Suit)
1. **Application / Petition Filing:** Submission before the local Tehsildar or Revenue Officer.
2. **Issuance of Proclamation:** Public notice inviting objections from villagers or co-sharers.
3. **Field Inquiry & Report:** Revenue Inspector (Patwari/Lekhpal) verification report.
4. **Hearing of Objections:** Adjudication by Revenue Officer.
5. **Order & Record Update:** Amendment of Record of Rights (ROR / Jamabandi).

* **Highest Volume Categories:** Mutation entries and illegal land encroachment removal applications.
* **Highest Pain Categories:** Corruption at the grassroots Patwari/Lekhpal level, manual and opaque land registers, and multi-tier appellate escalation up to the Board of Revenue.

---

## 8. Family Courts
* **Role:** Specialized courts designed for speedy settlement of matrimonial disputes and preservation of family units.

### Case Types
* **Matrimonial Relief:** Divorce petitions (Mutual Consent vs. Contested under Hindu Marriage Act, Special Marriage Act, Muslim Personal Law, etc.).
* **Maintenance & Alimony:** Claims under Section 125 CrPC / BNSS.
* **Child Custody & Guardianship:** Visitation rights and minor protection.

### User Journey (Contested Divorce & Maintenance)
1. **Petition Presentation:** Filing divorce/maintenance petition with counseling referral.
2. **Mandatory Counseling:** Sessions with court-attached marriage counselors.
3. **Interim Maintenance Application:** Financial disclosure affidavits filed by both spouses.
4. **Trial & Evidence:** Examination of witnesses regarding cruelty, desertion, or breakdown.
5. **Final Decree & Custody Orders:** Dissolution of marriage and permanent alimony settlement.

* **Highest Volume Categories:** Section 125 maintenance petitions and Section 498A (cruelty) linked matrimonial disputes.
* **Highest Pain Categories:** Emotional acrimony, protracted custody battles weaponizing children, and delayed interim maintenance adjudication leading to severe financial distress.

## 9. Synthesis & Strategic Insights for NYAYAOS Architecture
To build an effective digital infrastructure layer (`nyayos`), system design must target these core friction points:
1. **Interoperability:** Standardize case data flows from disparate revenue and quasi-judicial tribunals into the National Judicial Data Grid (NJDG) pipelines.
2. **Automated Document Scrutiny:** Implement AI-driven registry checks to eliminate repetitive filing defects across Supreme Court SLPs and High Court writs.
3. **Process Tracking for Execution:** Introduce specialized digital tracking modules for Consumer and Civil execution petitions, which represent the true bottleneck of "paper justice" in India.
