Helping parties arrive prepared — before any formal proceeding begins.

NyayOS is a dispute readiness platform for Indian businesses. It helps parties organise their evidence, timeline, and facts before they approach any forum.

Relevance to government mechanisms
MSME FCMSME Facilitation Councils require structured dispute briefs before conciliation. NyayOS helps parties prepare the organised package required to participate effectively.
ConsumerConsumer Commission complainants benefit from structured timelines and linked evidence before filing — reducing procedural delays caused by incomplete submissions.
ODR / ADROnline dispute resolution platforms require structured data inputs. NyayOS produces a shareable brief compatible with ODR intake formats.
DLSADistrict Legal Services Authorities can direct parties to NyayOS before Lok Adalat hearings to improve case quality and throughput.

Data sovereignty & privacy

NyayOS is controlled entirely by the party. No data is shared with any government body, court, or third party without explicit user consent. Parties retain the right to delete their file at any time.

NyayOS does not
  • Give legal advice or make legal determinations
  • File documents with any court or authority on behalf of a party
  • Represent parties in any proceeding
  • Share case content between parties without consent
  • Predict or influence outcomes
