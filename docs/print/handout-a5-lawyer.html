NyayOS helps clients organise their dispute before the first consultation — so you spend less time reconstructing the situation and more time on the law.

What a client brings from NyayOS
Confirmed timelineDate-by-date events, with certainty levels shown per entry.
Organised evidenceDocuments categorised and linked to specific facts.
Flagged contradictionsConflicts between sources, annotated by the client.
Missing evidence notedWhat they couldn't locate, with their explanation.

How sharing works

The client grants you secure, time-limited access to their structured dispute file. You receive facts with sources, evidence, and a timeline — not a verbal account. Access expires automatically. The client can revoke it at any time.

Assurances for legal professionals
  • NyayOS does not give legal advice or make legal determinations
  • Issue classification is provisional — client-confirmed only
  • AI extractions are always labelled and require client confirmation
  • NyayOS does not create attorney-client privilege
  • No data is shared with any third party without explicit client consent
  • Clients can correct or delete any information at any time
