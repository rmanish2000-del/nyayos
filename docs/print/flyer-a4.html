Your guide through what happened.
Something went wrong.
Now you need to be ready.

A payment dispute. An undelivered order. A contract that wasn't honoured. Most people don't know where to start — and most disputes go unresolved because they never get organised. NyayOS gives you the organised starting point that everything else depends on.

How it works — 5 steps
1
Tell your story
In your own words. No legal terms needed.
2
Add your documents
Contracts, invoices, messages, photos.
3
Confirm the facts
Review what was found. You decide what's correct.
4
See your options
Negotiation, mediation, tribunal, court — explained plainly.
5
Share when ready
Give a lawyer or advisor access. You control it.
NyayOS does
  • Organise your story into a clear record
  • Structure and categorise your documents
  • Build a date-by-date timeline of events
  • Flag contradictions without resolving them
  • Show you what evidence may be missing
  • Let you share your file securely
NyayOS does not
  • Give legal advice
  • Predict outcomes or tell you who will win
  • File anything on your behalf
  • Share your information without permission
  • Represent you in any proceeding
  • Replace a lawyer or mediator
Who it's for
Small businesses, vendors, service providers, and commercial parties with disputes involving payment, delivery, contracts, or service quality. Currently available by early access — contact us to participate.
