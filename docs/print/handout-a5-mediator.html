NyayOS
For Mediators & Arbitrators

Parties can arrive with a structured record — not just a story.

NyayOS helps parties organise their dispute before mediation or arbitration, so you spend less time reconstructing events and more time facilitating resolution.

What a party brings from NyayOS
Confirmed timelineDate-by-date events with source citations and certainty levels.
Structured evidenceDocuments categorised and linked to specific factual claims.
Flagged contradictionsConflicts between sources, noted with both versions preserved.
Gaps acknowledgedMissing evidence identified and explained by the party themselves.

Sharing with the proceeding

Each party controls their own NyayOS file independently. They may choose to share a structured brief with you as mediator/arbitrator. Sharing is voluntary, time-limited, and revocable. NyayOS does not share between parties without explicit consent from each.

Assurances for proceedings
  • NyayOS does not make legal determinations or fact findings
  • AI inferences are always labelled — never presented as confirmed facts
  • Every confirmed fact required explicit user action to approve
  • Parties can correct or delete any information at any time
  • NyayOS does not communicate between parties
  • No data is shared with third parties without explicit consent
  • NyayOS does not create any privilege or confidentiality obligation

Note on AI labelling: All AI-extracted content in a NyayOS file is visually marked as an inference until the user confirms it. Amber-labelled items are unconfirmed AI extractions; green-labelled items have been explicitly confirmed by the party.
